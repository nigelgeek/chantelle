Bonjour, and welcome!

I’m a French language tutor with a genuine love for the language and the opportunities it creates. My journey with French began many years ago out of a curiosity for the language and its rich culture. What started as an interest soon became a lifelong passion, leading me to pursue formal studies in French and continue developing my skills through years of learning and practical use.

Over the years, I’ve had the privilege of teaching French to learners at different stages and each experience has reinforced my belief that language learning should be enjoyable and practical. I love seeing how a new language can broaden perspectives and open doors to new friendships, travel, education and career opportunities.

I’m naturally patient, encouraging and enjoy creating a relaxed learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and making mistakes. Outside of teaching, I enjoy reading, exploring different cultures and travelling.

Whether your child is just starting their French journey or looking to build on existing skills, I’d be delighted to help them develop a lasting appreciation for the language while growing in confidence every step of the way.
